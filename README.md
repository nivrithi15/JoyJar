# JoyJar

> A little place for the moments that make life happier.

JoyJar is a digital memory jar designed to help people **capture, preserve, and revisit the small moments that bring them joy**.

Instead of letting meaningful memories disappear into a camera roll or forgotten notes, JoyJar turns them into a beautiful, interactive collection that users can return to whenever they need a little happiness.

---

## What is JoyJar?

We often remember the big moments — birthdays, vacations, achievements — but some of the happiest memories are the smallest ones:

> "My friend made me laugh today."
> "The sunset looked beautiful on my way home."
> "We stayed up talking until 2 AM."

JoyJar gives these little moments a place to live.

Users can:

* Save simple moments
* Create memories with photos
* Explore memories through an interactive glitter trail
* Build their personal happiness jar
* Rediscover random memories
* Create special "Open When" collections
* Watch their memories grow into a visual happiness garden

---

## Features

### Moments

A simple space to capture thoughts and memories without needing a photo.

Write down anything that made your day a little better.

### Memory Jar

Create richer memories by combining:

* Photos
* Text
* Dates
* Feelings

Memories are presented through an interactive glitter trail, making revisiting them feel like discovering little pieces of happiness.

### Glitter Trail

The signature JoyJar interaction.

Instead of displaying memories as a traditional list, memories are connected through an animated trail of sparkles.

Users can follow the trail and discover memories one by one.

### Open When

Create collections of memories for different moments:

* Open when you're sad
* Open when you need a little happiness
* Open when you miss someone
* Open when you need motivation
* Open on your birthday

A personal collection of happy memories whenever you need them.

### Surprise Me

Don't know what memory to revisit?

Press "Surprise Me" and JoyJar randomly brings one of your memories back.

> "A little memory found its way back to you."

### Happiness Garden

Every saved moment contributes to a growing visual garden.

The more memories you collect, the more your garden grows.

Your memories become a visual representation of the happiness you've collected over time.

---

## Why JoyJar?

Digital memories are often scattered across:

* Camera rolls
* Messaging apps
* Social media
* Notes apps

JoyJar brings them together into a space specifically designed around positive memories and meaningful moments.

The goal isn't to document everything.

It's to **save the things worth remembering.**

## Cloudinary Integration

Cloudinary powers JoyJar's visual memory experience.

When a user adds a photo:

```text
User uploads photo
       |
       v
   Cloudinary
       |
       v
Image stored and optimized
       |
       v
  Memory created
       |
       v
Photo appears in Memory Jar
       |
       v
Glitter Trail experience
```

Cloudinary allows JoyJar to efficiently manage and deliver the photos that make up a user's memory collection.

## Design Philosophy

JoyJar uses a warm, playful and dreamy visual language.

The interface is designed to feel:

* Whimsical
* Warm
* Joyful
* Soft
* Personal

Rather than feeling like a traditional productivity or journaling application, JoyJar is designed to feel like **opening a physical jar filled with tiny pieces of happiness.**

## How It Works

### 1. Capture

Save a moment that made you happy.

### 2. Personalize

Add a photo, text, date, and feeling.

### 3. Collect

Your memories become part of your personal JoyJar.

### 4. Revisit

Follow the glitter trail, open an old memory, or let JoyJar surprise you.

### 5. Grow

Over time, your collection becomes a visual representation of the happiness you've experienced.

In a world where we're constantly moving forward, JoyJar creates a small space to pause and look back at the moments that mattered.
